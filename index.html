How we build

A few promises every Little Mac Apps release keeps.

Truly native

Written in Swift for macOS, light on memory, and built with the controls, shortcuts and dark mode you already know.

Private by default

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Apps are sandboxed and only touch what you choose. Your files never leave your Mac.

Careful with your stuff

Nothing destructive happens without a clear confirmation, and anything removed goes to the Trash, so it can come back.

Fair pricing

Pay once on the Mac App Store. No subscriptions and no in-app upsells.

About

Little Mac Apps is an independent one-person studio, making a small number of Mac apps and looking after each one properly.

File Stacker is the first. The idea behind it is the idea behind the studio: tools that are honest about what they do, explain themselves, and treat your Mac with care. Every app is designed, built and supported by the same person, and every message gets read.
